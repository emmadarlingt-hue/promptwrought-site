States & rules

Hover on the primary swaps ink for hot-metal-deep — the accent appears on interaction, not at rest. Secondary inverts. Quiet darkens its underline. No lift, no scale, no shadow; 0.18s ease.
Hover on ink keeps the parchment fill and turns the label hot-metal-deep instead. Repainting the fill red on a dark surface drops the button’s edge to 2.5 : 1 against the band and it visually disappears.
Outlined buttons on ink take --control-edge-reversed (parchment at 55%, 4.3 : 1), not the 30% --rule-light used for dividers — a control’s edge needs 3 : 1 against its surface, and 30% only reaches 2.4 : 1. On light the equivalent is --control-edge at 3.5 : 1.
Focus is a 2px hot-metal-deep outline at 3px offset, used identically on links, buttons, and inputs (hot-metal-light when the surface is ink). Never remove it.
Labels are verbs in sentence case, uppercased by CSS — Subscribe, Read the archive, Submit a coinage. Never “Click here”, never “Learn more”.
Radius is zero everywhere. The system is set in rules and rectangles; a rounded corner reads as a different brand immediately.
Hit area on a button is at least 44 × 44px including padding, even at the small size. Category chips are the deliberate exception at 32px — they sit in dense rows where 44 would break the line rhythm. WCAG 2.2 SC 2.5.8 sets the AA target at 24 × 24px, which 32 clears comfortably; 44 is the AAA bar, and buttons keep it.
