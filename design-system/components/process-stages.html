Two acrosswider minmax for longer copy

I

What it is for

Any ordered sequence of two to six steps: an editorial process, a submission guide, a set of principles. Beyond six the grid stops reading as a sequence and becomes a list — use one instead.

II

What it is not for

Features, benefits, or pricing tiers. The numeral implies order; if the items aren’t ordered, the numeral is lying and the component is the wrong one.

Numerals are roman and unpunctuated — I, II, III, IV — in --hot-metal at 0.3em tracking. Headings are single gerunds where possible: Noticing, Digging, Striking, Sending. Body is one sentence, capped at about 90 characters, so all four columns bottom out near the same line.
