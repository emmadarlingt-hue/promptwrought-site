Two habits that make it the brand

The squeeze. Display type in the wordmark, headwords and section titles is horizontally scaled to scaleX(.94) with a left origin. It is what makes Cormorant look struck rather than typed. Apply it to display sizes only — never to body copy, labels, or anything under 24px.

The tracking floor. Utility type is 0.34em on desktop, eased to 0.26em under 48rem so it doesn’t shear apart on a phone. Set letter-spacing on the element, not the parent — the trailing space on the last character is intentional and part of the artwork’s rhythm.
