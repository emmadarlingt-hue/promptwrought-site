Layout & rhythm

One column, generous gutters, and horizontal rules doing the work that boxes and cards do elsewhere. Nothing on this site sits in a container with a shadow. It sits between two lines, like an entry.

Vertical rhythm is the body leading — 1.62. Paragraph spacing is 1.15rem, senses 1.35rem, section titles 1.25rem above their first paragraph. Grid gaps step in clamps of 1.75–2.5rem.

The bandthe only layout primitive

band · parchment

An eyebrow opens it

A band is a full-bleed horizontal slab with --band padding above and below and the wrap centred inside. It carries a surface colour and nothing else — no border radius, no shadow, no card.

band · parchment-deep

Shift the surface a shade to mark an aside — the editor’s note, a standfirst. Same padding, same wrap.

band · hot-metal-deep

One band per page may be filled with the accent — the call to subscribe. Use the deep variant so the parchment copy inside it clears AA.

band · ink

Ink closes the page. Reversed wordmark, one line of utility type, nothing else.

Measure66ch, and hold to it

The -wright suffix is one of the oldest job descriptions in English. It comes from Old English wyrhta, a worker or maker, and it survives now almost entirely in trades that have gone: the wheelwright, the shipwright, the millwright, the cartwright. Only the playwright is still at work.

Always pair the measure with text-wrap: pretty — it stops orphans in a serif face where a single short last line is very visible. Display headings additionally cap at 20–24ch.

Rules of motion

Only the first screen animates, and only three elements deep.
No parallax, no scroll-jacking, no hover lift.
Links underline on hover at 0.18em offset — that is the entire hover vocabulary.
The whole block sits inside @media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference), so reduced-motion users get the finished state with no fallback needed.
