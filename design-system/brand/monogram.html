Monogram

A capital P and a lowercase w on a shared baseline, separated by the hot-metal interpunct, inside a double rule. It reads as a maker’s stamp punched into the back of a piece — which is the whole idea.

Primary & reverseddouble rule: 4px outer, 1.5px inner, 9px apart

Size stepsthe frame thins, the name drops

Below 96px the PROMPTWROUGHT line is dropped — at that size it is texture, not text. Below 32px, drop the inner rule too and keep only the outer frame and the letters. The interpunct is the last thing to go; it never goes.

Where it goes

Use the monogram for the favicon, social avatars, the app icon, wax-seal moments at the end of an issue, and anywhere narrower than 120px.
Use the wordmark for the masthead, email headers, the footer, and anything where the name has room to be read.
Never lock the two together. They are alternates, not a combination mark.
